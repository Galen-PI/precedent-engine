TRIALS: confirmatory=0 descriptive=6 ledger_before=112    (no confirmatory test; six descriptive looks: the structural counts S0 and the five listings S1 to S5; ledger_before = SELECT COUNT(*) FROM flag_ledger)
HURDLE: this is not a statistical test, so the reference hurdle for a new effect (t>=3.0, ANCHORS.md item 3) does not apply and is written here only to satisfy the template. The pass line is the PASS TEST below.
DECAY: forward_fraction=1.0 because a data defect does not decay with time; the forward check is that the frozen thresholds are applied to the January 2027 data and, mechanically, to the sealed tickers.
STANDARD TEST: PART A, structural checks (counts, then every row if the count is at most 100): rows dated on a day that is not a SPY trading day; null or non-positive prices; high below low; close outside the day range by more than 0.5 percent; adjusted_close different from close; duplicate stock-days. PART B, moves: the view g4_part_b_candidates, a one-day adjusted return of 15 percent or more AND a move relative to SPY of at least 6 times the stock own 64-day volatility (712 rows by the counts), listed with the move, the volume ratio, the nearest change point of price_adjustment_factors within 3 days, the number of 8-K filings within 5 calendar days and the nearest event. PART C, the adjustment-factor check: every change point whose ratio jumps by a factor k with abs ln k of 0.10 or more AND whose day has a stored move of 10 percent or more (18 rows by the counts), with the implied as-traded return (1 plus the stored return, divided by k, minus 1; this assumes as-traded equals stored divided by ratio, an inference tested by p8). REVIEW TIERS: tier 1a all of PART C; tier 1b all PART B candidates with a move of 30 percent or more (93); tier 1c all of 15 to 30 percent with no 8-K within 5 days (about 102); tier 2 the 15 to 30 percent group with an 8-K within 5 days (about 517): a sample of 60 in the fixed order of md5(ticker and date), and if 3 or more of the 60 are artifacts (5 percent) ALL of tier 2 is reviewed. Each reviewed item gets a label (artifact, real, uncertain), the evidence used, and Galen confirms or corrects every item; uncertain items stay uncertain. PASS TEST (G4 passes if all hold): (1) every PART A count above zero is listed and handled (excluded or explained), or the check is declared unusable under rule 1 of WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE PLAN; (2) every tier 1 item and the tier 2 sample (or all of tier 2 after escalation) is reviewed and labeled; (3) every artifact whose date lies inside the 20-day window of an event that is in the analysis set is proposed for event_exclusions and the user decides; (4) the positive controls are caught; and a sensitivity statement says how many reaction rows each artifact touches.
PLACEBO: positive controls: KDP 2018-07-10, KLAC 2014-11-26 and HPQ 2015-11-02 (found by the old scan) must appear in tier 1a or 1b; the tier 2 sample is drawn in a fixed md5 order so it cannot be tuned. No outcome-based placebo exists for a data-quality scan; that is a limit, not a feature.
FORWARD CHECK: after the review, the same thresholds are applied mechanically (counts only, no candidate read) to the sealed tickers and to the January 2027 data at runbook step 7; the sealed candidates are read only when Galen decides.
PREDICTIONS: ranges are 80 percent intervals (6 numeric rows, so about 1 is expected to miss); the probability rows state yes or no events; scoring uses scripts/ops/score_predictions.py after the review. The tier sizes (about 18, 93, 102, 517) are deterministic from the counts and are not predictions.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE PLAN: (1) if the high-low check (close outside the day range by more than 0.5 percent) fails on 1 percent of rows or more, it is declared unusable (the high and low may be on another scale than the stored close) and reported as such. (2) If a positive control is NOT in tier 1a or 1b: stop and diagnose the rule before reading any other candidate. (3) If the implied as-traded returns of KDP and KLAC are not within 3 points of -82 and -20 percent, the reading of the ratio is wrong: the implied column is discarded and reported. (4) If a listing returns exactly 100 rows it is truncated: split it and rerun before reading. (5) 3 or more artifacts among the 60 sampled tier 2 candidates: review all of tier 2. (6) If more than 100 artifacts are found, re-plan the review capacity with Galen before proposing exclusions.
LIMITS: non-sealed tickers only. The review has no external corporate-action data: a label rests on the stored series, the change-point table, volume, nearby filings and events, and the assistant own knowledge of well-known corporate actions, which is marked as unverified memory wherever it is used. The reading of the ratio (as-traded equals stored divided by ratio) is an inference tested by p8. The 8-K filter uses filing dates within 5 calendar days, so a move near any 8-K counts as near an event, including routine ones. The thresholds (15 percent and z of 6) came from the counts of 2026-10-30; artifacts smaller than 15 percent or below z of 6 stay unmeasured and would need their own registration. Real moves can be mislabeled in either direction. Stock-days are not independent (one event can produce several candidates), so shares are shares of reviewed items, not of independent cases.

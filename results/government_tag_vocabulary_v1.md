# GOVERNMENT-DECISION TAG VOCABULARY v1 (2026-10-23; committed BEFORE any tagging call). The buckets that become THREADS: every tag is one row (decision, bucket_type, bucket, direction, confidence, evidence, vocab_version, model_version) in a long-form table, so a thread is a (bucket_type, bucket) pair that joins to events, tests and the ledger. New buckets are added by a NEW vocabulary version; old tags keep theirs.
BUCKET TYPES.
 sector (0-4 per decision): Communication Services, Consumer Discretionary, Consumer Staples, Energy, Financials, Health Care, Industrials, Information Technology, Materials, Real Estate, Utilities (the 11 values already in securities.sector). Each carries a DIRECTION: benefit | harm | mixed | unclear.
 theme (1-3 per decision): trade_tariffs, sanctions_foreign_policy, taxation, budget_appropriations, financial_regulation, banking_monetary, healthcare_drug_pricing, environment_climate, energy_policy, technology_ai_data, telecom_media, labor_employment, antitrust_competition, defense_procurement, housing_real_estate, immigration, agriculture_food, transportation_infrastructure, consumer_protection, government_operations.
 scope (exactly 1): market_wide | sector_specific | company_specific | not_company_relevant.
 persistence (exactly 1): one_off | persistent_condition (a standing condition until the next decision on the subject; the buff/debuff framing).
EVIDENCE RULE: every sector and theme tag must carry a QUOTED phrase (at most 25 words) copied from the supplied text; a tag without a verifiable quote is invalid. A decision whose supplied text is only a title (no source text) is tagged at confidence 'low' and may not receive sector tags.
CONFIDENCE: high | medium | low.
DETERMINISTIC BASELINE (laws only; a free consistency check, NOT an input to the tagger): Congress.gov's own policy_area maps to a default theme. Agriculture and Food -> agriculture_food; Armed Forces and National Security -> defense_procurement; Commerce -> consumer_protection (weak); Congress -> government_operations; Crime and Law Enforcement -> government_operations; Economics and Public Finance -> budget_appropriations; Education -> government_operations (weak); Emergency Management -> government_operations; Energy -> energy_policy; Environmental Protection -> environment_climate; Families -> government_operations (weak); Finance and Financial Sector -> financial_regulation; Foreign Trade and International Finance -> trade_tariffs; Government Operations and Politics -> government_operations; Health -> healthcare_drug_pricing; Housing and Community Development -> housing_real_estate; Immigration -> immigration; International Affairs -> sanctions_foreign_policy; Labor and Employment -> labor_employment; Law -> government_operations; Native Americans -> government_operations; Public Lands and Natural Resources -> environment_climate (weak); Science, Technology, Communications -> technology_ai_data; Social Welfare -> government_operations; Taxation -> taxation; Transportation and Public Works -> transportation_infrastructure; Water Resources Development -> transportation_infrastructure; no default for Animals, Arts Culture Religion, Civil Rights and Liberties Minority Issues, Social Sciences and History, Sports and Recreation.
VALIDITY BAR (registered before the first call): a hand-read of 150 random tagged decisions (stratified: laws and orders in proportion, three eras) plus every tag marked low confidence in a further 100; a SECTOR or THEME tag counts correct if the quote supports it and a careful reader would agree; the vocabulary is usable if the sector-tag precision is at least 80% with a Wilson lower bound of at least 70% and the theme-tag precision at least 85%; otherwise the prompt is revised ONCE and the read repeated on a fresh sample; nothing is bulk-confirmed.

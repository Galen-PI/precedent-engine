# PRE-REGISTRATION 2026-10-23 (committed BEFORE the first real run): is the whole market's move larger on the days an FOMC decision becomes public?
SCRIPT: scripts/card/fomc_day_test.py (read-only). DATA: fomc_meetings (loaded 2026-10-23 from the Fed's own pages; announcement_date = the day the decision was public), macro_long (DFEDTAR to 2008-12-15, DFEDTARU / DFEDTARL after, to flag target changes), sector_day_z (market-wide statistic from 2004, where the 64-day volatility baseline exists).
STATISTIC. Identical to eo_day_test.py: MKT(t) = n-weighted mean of the 11 sectors' mean |return| / prior 64-day volatility; for an announcement day i, LD = log MKT(i) - mean log MKT over baseline days 21-63 trading days away, excluding EVERY announcement day and the day after (the full set X); AMPLIFICATION = exp(mean LD); SE = sd / sqrt(days). An announcement on a weekend or holiday maps to the next trading day (bisect_left), as in eo_day_test.py. Day -1 is the same statistic applied to the day before (baseline measured from that day).
PRIMARY POPULATION. SCHEDULED meetings with a statement (kind = meeting, not flagged UNSCHEDULED, announcement_date present), 2004-2026, day 0. Eras: A 2004-2014, B 2015-2026, B1 = B without 2020.
PRIMARY RULE (as eo_day_test.py). PASS: amplification above 1 with t >= 2.5 in BOTH eras A and B, both +21 / -21 day placebos clean (|t| < 2.5), AND B1 above 1 with t >= 2.0. LEAD: t >= 2.0 in both eras A and B (placebos clean). Otherwise NULL.
DESCRIPTIVE ONLY (no pass line; many looks, so exploratory): day -1 and day +1 for the scheduled meetings; scheduled meetings split into HOLD and CHANGE (a change = the target-rate midpoint differs on the announcement day or the next day); UNSCHEDULED announcements (conference calls and meetings flagged UNSCHEDULED) at day 0; sub-eras 2004-2007, 2008-2014, 2015-2019, 2020-2026.
PREDICTIONS (point and range): scheduled day 0 amplification A 1.12 (1.04-1.25), B 1.10 (1.03-1.22); P(PASS) 55%, P(LEAD, not PASS) 15%; day -1 0.97 (0.92-1.02); day +1 1.04 (0.98-1.12); CHANGE days 1.20 (1.05-1.45) vs HOLD days 1.07 (1.00-1.15); UNSCHEDULED day 0 1.8 (1.3-3.0); scheduled meetings in the sector-day range: about 180 (160-190).
LIMITS: a daily |return| captures only part of a 2:00 pm announcement's reaction; announcements can coincide with other news; unscheduled actions cluster in crises (2008, 2020), so they are descriptive; 2020-03 dominates the unscheduled group; amplification is the size of the move, not its direction (the direction question, e.g. pre-FOMC drift, needs signed returns and is a separate registration).

# PREREG 2026-10-26 (written BEFORE the all-ticker scan's ticker list was read): a per-ticker "levels valid from" date for the as-quoted prices
FINDING SO FAR (hand-read, two tickers, vendor adjust=none fetched directly, not our reconstruction): the vendor's "unadjusted" series is not penny-exact before a corporate action. DRI: regime change 2015-11-10 (close -11.03%, adjusted/unadjusted ratio 0.65223 -> 0.72964); before it closes carry four decimals and sit on and off the penny grid at about the chance rate, after it they are clean pennies. GL: event 2013-05-03 (close +20.77%, ratio x5/6 exactly, 7.13813 -> 5.94844); pre-event levels near $5 for a stock that traded near $60; clean pennies from 2013-05-10. Per-year grid share (check_asquoted_accuracy.py): DRI about 40% (chance) through 2014, 50% in 2015, 100% from 2016; GL about 40% through 2012, 80% in 2013, 100% from 2014. n = 2 tickers: this is a mechanism seen twice, not a rate.
RULE. Eligible closes: as-quoted close of at least $1; dated before 2001-01-01 (grid: multiples of 1/32 within $0.004, chance 25.6%) or from 2001-06-01 (grid: whole cents within $0.002, chance 40%); closes from 2001-01-01 to 2001-05-31 skipped (decimalization transition). A WINDOW is 20 consecutive eligible closes; a BAD window has an on-grid share below 70% (under chance, a 20-close window reaches 70% on-grid well under 1% of the time; clean data is 100%). VALID_FROM = the first eligible close after the end of the ticker's LAST bad window; a ticker with no bad window is valid from its first as-quoted day; a ticker whose last bad window ends inside its final 250 closes is NEVER VALID; a ticker with fewer than 20 eligible closes is UNASSESSED. The rule is conservative by design: windows straddling an event stay bad until about 14 clean closes have accumulated.
USE. Level-dependent analyses (round-number anchoring, price thresholds, dollar moves, any as-quoted level) use only closes on or after valid_from and report how many tickers and closes the rule removed; analyses that use returns from the adjusted prices are unaffected. The five tickers already flagged in the factor table (ARE, BNY, CTVA, GEHC, JCI) keep their own exclusions in addition.
PREDICTIONS (point and probability). DRI valid_from between 2015-11-10 and 2015-12-31: 85%. GL valid_from between 2013-05-03 and 2013-06-30: 85%. Tickers with valid_from after 2002-01-01 (a real post-2001 loss): between 8 and 40 of 492: 60% (extrapolating 2 of 60 gives about 16). Tickers NEVER VALID: at most 3: 80%. Share of all eligible closes removed by the rule: at most 5%: 75%. The all-ticker scan finds 8 to 25 tickers below 97% post-2001: 60% (unchanged).
WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE PLAN. More than 15% of tickers with valid_from after 2005: level analyses would be restricted so heavily that an independent source of as-traded closes would be needed instead of this vendor's feed. Fewer than 5 affected tickers: the rule is applied as written and recorded. A window of clean data that the rule marks bad (a real price on the grid but a bad window by chance, which should occur well under 1% of the time): reported, and the 70% line is not moved after the fact.
NOT IN THIS NOTE. The mechanism behind the vendor's seams (a 6:5 step that coincides with a +20.8% day) is not established; only its footprint is used. The earlier idea of refetching bad tickers with a tighter tolerance is withdrawn: the error is in the vendor's feed, not in our factors.

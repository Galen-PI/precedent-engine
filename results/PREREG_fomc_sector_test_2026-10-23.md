# PRE-REGISTRATION 2026-10-23 (committed BEFORE the first real run): do rate-sensitive sectors move MORE than the other sectors around scheduled FOMC announcements?
SCRIPT: scripts/card/fomc_day_sector_test.py (read-only). Same populations, statistic and baseline as the market-wide test (fomc_day_test.py, PASS 2026-10-23): scheduled meetings with a statement from 2004, baseline days 21-63 away excluding EVERY announcement day (scheduled and unscheduled) and the day after; per-sector series built exactly as fed_change_day_test.py builds them (log_ratio of each sector's mean z).
FIXED GROUPING (decided before looking at any sector number): RATE = Financials, Real Estate, Utilities; REST = the other eight sectors. WINDOW (primary) = day 0 and day +1 averaged per meeting; this choice is motivated by the market-wide result (day +1 larger than day 0) and is therefore post hoc in origin.
STATISTIC. For each scheduled meeting i: LDw(s) = mean of the log-differences at day 0 and day +1 for sector s; DELTA_i = mean over the 3 RATE sectors minus mean over the 8 REST sectors (same meeting, so paired); RATIO = exp(mean DELTA); t = mean DELTA / (sd / sqrt(meetings)). Eras A 2004-2014, B 2015-2026.
RULE. PASS: RATIO >= 1.05 with t >= 2.5 in BOTH eras and the +21 / -21 shifted placebo of DELTA clean (|t| < 2.5) in both. LEAD: RATIO > 1.03 with t >= 2.0 in both eras. REVERSE: RATIO <= 0.95 with t <= -2.5 in both eras (rate-sensitive sectors move LESS than the rest). Otherwise NULL.
DESCRIPTIVE ONLY (11 sectors x 2 windows x 2 eras = many looks, exploratory): each sector's day 0, day +1 and combined amplification per era.
PREDICTIONS (point and range): RATIO A 1.06 (0.98-1.15), B 1.05 (0.97-1.14); P(PASS) 30%, P(LEAD, not PASS) 15%, P(NULL) 50%, P(REVERSE) 5%; Financials the largest single sector in at least one era (60%).
LIMITS: sector series are noisier than the market-wide one (resolution of a ratio is about 5-6% per era); the grouping is conventional, not tested against alternatives (that would be a search); sectors use the CURRENT GICS assignment (survivorship); size not direction; day_card.py frozen.
# ADDENDUM 1 (2026-10-23, after the first run, before any further sector test): fed_change_day_test.log_ratio (lines 68-75) builds each sector's series RELATIVE to the n-weighted mean z of the OTHER sectors: lr = log(MZ[k] / rest). The registered RATE-minus-REST contrast was therefore computed on relative series, and the registered descriptive table shows who moves more than the rest of the market (its eleven values average about 0.99 against a market-wide 1.14, which first revealed it). The verdict (NULL) was computed as registered and STANDS. A re-run of the same contrast on ABSOLUTE series (each sector's own mean z against its own baseline, scripts/card/fomc_day_sector_absolute.py, descriptive) gave the same answer: 1.040 [0.999, 1.082] t +1.91 (2004-2014) and 1.002 [0.965, 1.039] t +0.08 (2015-2026).

# PREREG 2026-10-26: round-number price anchoring (two tests), written BEFORE any outcome was computed
DATA AND USE RULE. results/PREREG_asquoted_levels_valid_from_2026-10-26.md addendum 6: closes from 2001-06-01, tickers with status valid (rule_version since_2001-06-01; 491, HON excluded), each from its valid_from; as-quoted close = stored adjusted close / the latest change-point ratio. Outcome-free counts: SCRIPTS.md addendum 20 (scripts/ingestion/round_number_counts.py). OUTCOME SOURCE for test 2: stock_day_z (security_id uuid, day date, z real; z = |return| / the standard deviation of the previous 64 returns, the project's move-size unit; coverage listed by an outcome-free query after this registration; the notes of 2026-10-16 say 2004-01-01 onward). GUARD: days with fewer than 100 stocks in stock_day_z are dropped (the same guard as fed_change_day_test.py; five stray non-trading dates are known). ERAS: A = before 2014-01-01, B = from 2014-01-01 (for test 2: the days available in stock_day_z).
TEST 1, CLUSTERING OF CLOSES. Unit: a valid close of at least $10 whose distance to the nearest cent is at most $0.002 (others are excluded and their share reported); c = its cents value, 0 to 99. Baseline cents = the 80 values that are not multiples of 5 (multiples of 5 cluster in all stocks). For a class of cents values, f = the share of closes per value; RATIO = f(class) / mean f(baseline). PRIMARY: RATIO of c = 0 (whole dollars), closes of at least $10 pooled, per era. SECONDARY (descriptive, with intervals, never promoted to a pass): c = 50; c in {25, 75}; the other multiples of 5; by price bucket $10-20, $20-50, $50-100, $100-250, $250 and above; closes of $5-10. INFERENCE: bootstrap over TICKERS (2,000 resamples; a block is all of one ticker's closes; 95% interval = the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles). CALIBRATION FROM NOISE: for each of the 80 baseline cents values the ratio of its share to the mean baseline share, with the same bootstrap; the share of them whose interval contains 1.0 must be at least 90%, else EVERY interval of test 1 is widened by the factor (95th percentile of |ratio - 1| / bootstrap SE across the 80 values) / 1.96 before the pass line is applied. PASS (the pattern exists at a size worth modelling): RATIO(c = 0) at least 1.05 with a lower interval bound of at least 1.03 in BOTH eras. LEAD: lower bound above 1.00 in both eras. Otherwise NULL (the estimates are reported anyway).
TEST 2, BEHAVIOUR NEAR ROUND THRESHOLDS. Zones exactly as defined in round_number_counts.py: previous valid close P in [$10, $100) (class s10); round threshold k x 10 with k = 2..9 (interior); placebo neighbours at (k - 0.27) x 10 (kind P73) and (k + 0.27) x 10 (kind P27); a day is in a zone when P is within 1% of the threshold, below or above it. OUTCOME: z on day t (the day AFTER P). For each era and k: RHO_k = mean z in the round zones / the mean of (mean z in the P27 zones, mean z in the P73 zones). ESTIMATE = ln RHO pooled over k with weights w_k = the round zone-days of k in the era (fixed from the full sample). INFERENCE: bootstrap over tickers as in test 1 (2,000 resamples, whole tickers); t = estimate / bootstrap SE; always report the percent change in mean z with its 95% interval. CALIBRATION: the same pooled estimate for the placebo contrast ln(mean z in P27 zones / mean z in P73 zones) must show |t| below 2.5 in each era. YEAR CONSISTENCY: the sign of the pooled estimate by calendar year equals the era's sign in at least 70% of that era's years. PASS: the estimate has the same sign in both eras with |t| at least 2.5 in each, the placebo contrast is clean in each era, and year consistency holds. LEAD: |t| at least 2.0 in both eras with the same sign and a clean placebo contrast. Otherwise NULL. ALWAYS REPORT, descriptive: the estimate and its 95% interval per era; for the below and the above zones separately; per k.
SECONDARY AND EXCLUDED CELLS (from the counts only): the s100 class in era B with k = 2..7 is descriptive with intervals; s1, s1000 and s100 in era A are not analysed (too thin); the DECADE thresholds ($10, $100, $1,000) have one-sided neighbours and need their own registration. NOT A TEST: the occupancy observation of addendum 20 (round zones hold about 5% fewer zone-days than their placebo neighbours) is exploratory; nothing here tests it.
MULTIPLICITY AND LOOKS: two confirmatory tests, each with ONE primary statistic per era and a pass line that needs both eras; every other statistic is labelled descriptive; the ledger rows will record how many descriptive statistics were looked at.
PREDICTIONS (point and 95% range; convention of SCRIPTS.md consolidated note 4, item D: scored as the distance in standard errors and interval overlap; verdict probabilities stay sharp). TEST 1: RATIO(c = 0), closes of at least $10, era A point 1.06 (range 1.01 to 1.12), era B point 1.04 (1.00 to 1.09); there is no data anchor, only the literature on closes after decimalization (clustering weaker for liquid large caps than for small caps), so the ranges are wide. Verdict: PASS 40%, LEAD (not pass) 35%, NULL 25%. RATIO(c = 0) larger in era A than in era B: 60%. RATIO(c = 50) above 1.0 in both eras: 75%. RATIO for c in {25, 75} above 1.0 in both eras: 70%. Calibration share of the 80 baseline cents with an interval containing 1.0 at least 90%: 55%. TEST 2: estimate (ln RHO) era A point 0.000 (range -0.020 to +0.020), era B point 0.000 (-0.020 to +0.020). Verdict: PASS 5%, LEAD 15%, NULL 80%. Placebo contrast clean in both eras: 70%. Percent change in mean z for round versus placebo zones within +-3% in both eras: 80%. Reason: every direction test so far is null and event-free move-size effects near thresholds have no strong prior; the point of test 2 is that it is cheap and its null is informative for the card.
WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE PLAN. A failed clustering calibration widens the intervals by the stated factor; if the widened lower bound for c = 0 falls below 1.00 in an era, the test is NULL there. If the guard removes more than 20% of an era's zone-days, test 2 is reported as underpowered, not as null. If the cents filter drops more than 3% of the closes, the outcome script stops and the loss is read first. If stock_day_z lacks a date for more than 5% of the zone-days of an era, that is reported before any result.
LIMITS (written in advance): (1) the bootstrap resamples tickers, not days, so cross-sectional shocks on the same day are covered only by the year-consistency check and the placebo contrast; (2) z standardizes by the 64-day volatility, which includes the days around earlier crossings; (3) the universe is today's large-cap survivors, whose round-number behaviour may differ from small caps; (4) the uniform-cents null of test 1 is an approximation, which is why the baseline excludes multiples of 5; (5) a stock sitting near a threshold for weeks produces dependent zone-days, handled only by whole-ticker resampling.

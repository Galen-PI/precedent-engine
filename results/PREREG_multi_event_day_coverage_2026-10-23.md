# PRE-REGISTRATION 2026-10-23 (committed BEFORE the first real run): coverage of the event band on stock-days with TWO OR MORE labeled events
SCRIPT: scripts/card/event_band_multi_event_coverage.py. It reuses event_band_class_coverage.py (commit 18f5c1d) unchanged for the matrices, the band unit, the model, the edge (band_calibration.json, group "recorded event, no earnings"), the earnings-window exclusion and the eras (2005-2012, 2013-2019, 2020-2026).
QUESTION. That audit measures only stock-days with exactly ONE labeled event ("ambiguous days are left out"). On a stock-day with two or more (an announcement and its follow-up, or an event on the day after another, since day +1 of A is day 0 of B) the card uses the LEAD event's multiplier alone: day_card.own_events sorts by (off, event_id) and lead = events[0], i.e. day-0 events first, then the smaller event_id. Is the band calibrated there?
POPULATION. Stock-days from 2005 with >= 2 DISTINCT labeled events (ev_ent, non-bundled company events), outside the earnings window, with a finite band unit. MEASURE. Covered = band unit / m_lead <= edge, m_lead = the card's own band_multiplier for the lead event (type, size, timing, day offset), as the audit does for single events.
PRIMARY RULE (the audit's own rule, applied to ALL multi-event stock-days). UNDER-COVERED if in EVERY era there are >= 300 stock-days, coverage < 86% and the 95% Wilson interval's upper end < 90%; OVER-COVERED if coverage > 94% in every era with the lower end > 90%; CALIBRATED if every era has >= 300 stock-days and coverage inside 86-94%; CANNOT TEST if an era has < 300; otherwise UNCLEAR.
DESCRIPTIVE (no pass line): the same table for 2 events vs 3 or more, same event type vs mixed types, lead on day 0 vs day +1; and the IMPLIED EXTRA MULTIPLIER per era = the 90th percentile of (band unit / m_lead) divided by the edge (the factor on top of the lead's multiplier that would give 90% coverage). Single-event coverage is printed as a reference and a self-check (kept single event-days vs the recorded 36,923; edge vs the recorded 1.337).
PREDICTIONS (point and range): multi-event stock-days kept 7,000 (2,000-25,000); pooled coverage 85% (80-89); P(UNDER-COVERED) 40%, P(CALIBRATED) 35%, P(UNCLEAR) 20%, P(OVER-COVERED) 5%; pooled implied extra multiplier 1.08 (1.00-1.20); P(coverage for 3+ events is lower than for exactly 2) 70%.
LIMITS. A multi-event day includes adjacent-day overlaps, which are common in clustered news; event types and sizes differ across the pairs; the lead choice among ties is arbitrary (smallest event_id). This is a diagnostic: nothing on the card changes from it. If UNDER-COVERED, the follow-up (a separate pre-registered walk-forward test, as for the role bands) would compare the lead-only multiplier with a larger-of-the-multipliers rule and an additive event-count term, fitted on earlier eras and scored on later ones.
